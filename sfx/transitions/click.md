# [click.md](http://click.md)

name: click  
duration_seconds: 0.23  
category: cut  
intensity: low  
frequency_budget: high

## Description

A short, clean, digital click.

Feels precise, modern, and informational rather than cinematic. The sound creates a sense of progress and momentum without drawing attention to itself.

The listener should barely notice the sound consciously.

## Best Uses

- Talking-head jump cuts
- Educational content
- AI tutorials
- Coding tutorials
- Business content
- List items
- Bullet points
- Caption appearances
- UI animations

## Avoid Using For

- Emotional reveals
- Cinematic moments
- Product reveals
- Before/after transformations
- Slow dramatic scenes

## Editing Intent

Use this sound to maintain pacing.

When the speaker moves to a new idea, sentence, or step, this sound can reinforce the cut without making the edit feel aggressive.

## Example Timeline

0.0s "Step one..."  
CLICK

2.1s "Open ChatGPT..."  
CLICK

4.5s "Paste this prompt..."  
CLICK

## Usage Frequency

Recommended usage:  
70-80% of all cuts.

This should be the default cut sound for educational and informational content.

## AI Decision Rule

IF:

- informational cut
- talking-head cut
- sentence transition
- list progression

THEN:  
use click
