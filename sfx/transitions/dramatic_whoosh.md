# dramatic_[whoosh.md](http://whoosh.md)

name: dramatic_whoosh  
duration_seconds: 1.92  
category: reveal  
intensity: high  
frequency_budget: low

## Description

A long cinematic build followed by a powerful impact.

Creates anticipation and importance.

This sound communicates:

"Pay attention. Something important is about to happen."

## Best Uses

- Hook moments
- Major reveals
- Before/after transformations
- Case study outcomes
- Success stories
- Emotional moments
- Large visual transitions

## Avoid Using For

- Normal cuts
- Sentence transitions
- Educational pacing
- Frequent scene changes

Overuse quickly makes content feel exaggerated.

## Editing Intent

Reserve for moments that genuinely deserve emphasis.

The viewer should hear this sound only a few times per video.

## Example Timeline

"This student had never written code..."

DRAMATIC WHOOSH

"...three months later launched an AI app."

## Usage Frequency

Recommended usage:  
0-5% of cuts.

Maximum:  
1-3 uses per reel.

## AI Decision Rule

IF:

- major reveal
- transformation
- outcome moment
- emotional payoff
- strongest hook moment

THEN:  
use dramatic_whoosh

ELSE:  
prefer click or whoosh
